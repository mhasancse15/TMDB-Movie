# Flutter Rules

- Prefer `const` constructors/widgets when possible.
- Keep build methods readable.
- Extract reusable widgets.
- Avoid business logic inside widgets.
- Use theme and design-system values.
- Handle loading, error, empty, and success states where relevant.
- Use lazy builders for potentially large lists.
- Dispose controllers/listeners when ownership requires it.
- Avoid unnecessary `setState` or rebuilds.
